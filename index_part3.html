Weekly Home Activity / ہفتہ وار سرگرمی

This week's theme: "Water: Blessing of Allah / پانی: اللہ کی نعمت"

Project: Make a Floating Paper Flower!

Cut a paper flower with folded petals. Place it gently on a bowl of water and watch it bloom on its own!

Parent Steps: Fold petal edges toward center, float on water bowl, observe capillary action.

Learning Goal: Science of water, fine-motor scissor practice.
